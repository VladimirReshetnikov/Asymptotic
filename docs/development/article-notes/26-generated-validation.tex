\section{Independent generated validation and reproducible failures}
\label{sec:generated-validation}

The deterministic tests in \texttt{GeneratedAdvancedOracles.wlt} complement
the radical inverse pairs in \texttt{GeneratedInverseOracles.wlt}. Their
oracle is a separate finite symbolic substitution calculation. It does not
call the package's coefficient formula, sparse jets, multi-index enumeration,
Newton engine, or residual checker. Agreement between package methods remains
useful supplementary evidence; it is not the expected-value calculation in
these tests.

Write a generated local model as
\[
 f(u)=a u^p\left(1+\sum_{i=1}^{m}u^{\delta_i}B_i(\log u)\right),
 \qquad p\ne0,\quad \delta_i>0,
\]
and put $u=z(1+V)$. Introduce independent variables $t_i$ and a marker
$\epsilon$. The oracle solves the finite Taylor problem
\[
 (1+V)^p-1+
 \epsilon\sum_i t_i(1+V)^{p+\delta_i}
 B_i\bigl(L+\log(1+V)\bigr)=0,
 \qquad V=\sum_{n=1}^{N}v_n\epsilon^n.
\]
At marker order $n$, the new coefficient $v_n$ occurs with coefficient $p$.
All other terms are determined by earlier coefficients. Native
\wl{Series} and ordinary polynomial coefficient extraction therefore give
a triangular scalar recurrence, independently of the Euler--Lagrange
formula. The oracle checks both the coefficient of the new unknown and the
vanishing of the substituted forward residual through order $N$.

For an observable $u^r$, it then expands $(1+V)^r$. Each monomial
$t^k$ acquires the exact weight $k\cdot\delta$ under the substitution
$t_i=z^{\delta_i}$. To retain all weights strictly below $H$, it suffices to
choose
\[
 N=\left\lceil\frac{H}{\min_i\delta_i}\right\rceil-1.
\]
Indeed, an omitted monomial has total marker degree at least $N+1$ and hence
weight at least $(N+1)\min_i\delta_i\ge H$. The routine fixtures use small
depths; the independent oracle explicitly refuses depths greater than six.
Equal algebraic weights are combined by ordinary exact symbolic arithmetic
when the expected finite expression is compared with \wl{Normal} of the
public inverse object. No numerical tolerance decides equality or ordering.

A fixed sixteen-family cycle ensures coverage of rational logarithmic
polynomials, two and three irrational gaps, quadratic logarithmic
amplitudes, exact collisions, and cancellation. For example, with
$p=r=1$ the coefficients at the first three relative powers of
$u(1+c u+2c^2u^2-u^3)$ include a cancelled second-order block.
For two independent irrational gaps $\alpha,\beta$, the model
\[
 u\left(1+A u^\alpha+B u^\beta+
 (2+\alpha+\beta)AB\,u^{\alpha+\beta}\right)
\]
has cancelling inverse contributions at weight $\alpha+\beta$ from
different marker degrees. Both examples test complete weight blocks,
rather than counting contributions before they are combined.

Other families exercise source translation, the left branch, reciprocal
source coordinates at both infinities, signed leading coefficients, nonunit
leading powers, and observable powers. Declared forward remainders are
tested at their transported precision boundary and beyond it; the latter
must return \wl{Failure["InsufficientInputOrder",\ldots]}. An intentionally
insufficient index budget must return \wl{Failure["ResourceLimit",\ldots]}.
The exact coordinate identities
\[
\begin{aligned}
 y=b+a\log\log x \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 &x=\exp\exp\frac{y-b}{a},\\
 y=b+a e^{-1/x^2} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 &x=\frac1{\sqrt{-\log((y-b)/a)}}.
\end{aligned}
\]
are checked on their stated positive real branches, including a zero
remainder. They do not depend on the recurrence oracle.

The standalone script \texttt{RunGeneratedCampaign.wl} uses seed
$236369$ and the Mersenne Twister by default. Its default twenty-four
cases are separate from the sixteen-case routine suite. Environment
variables with prefix \texttt{ASYMPTOTIC\_CAMPAIGN\_} select
\texttt{SEED}, \texttt{CASES}, \texttt{CASE\_SECONDS},
\texttt{SHRINK\_ATTEMPTS}, \texttt{SHRINK\_SECONDS}, and
\texttt{OUTPUT}. Explicit finite bounds are enforced: at most ten thousand
cases, six hundred seconds per case or shrink pass, and one thousand shrink
attempts per failed case. A requested output directory must be new.

Before testing, the runner writes the complete exact inputs and a manifest
containing the seed, generator and oracle revision, actual kernel version,
system identifier, resource limits, and SHA-256 digests of the runner and
every kernel module. Each case writes its original input, requested operation,
expected and observed expressions, messages, and elapsed time before
shrinking begins. Timeouts, oracle failures, unexpected package failures,
incorrect coefficients, and violated rejection contracts remain distinct
outcomes. An interruption after an original case record has been written
does not erase that record.

The deterministic shrinker tries deleting perturbations, lowering logarithmic
degree, simplifying exact coefficients and powers, decreasing the requested
cutoff, and simplifying positive gaps. It accepts a candidate only when an
explicit lexicographic tuple of nonnegative integer complexity measures
decreases and the same failure class recurs. It preserves the original case,
writes every accepted reduction separately, and stops at its time or attempt
budget. Every completed shrink attempt also records its input and result,
including a different failure class that cannot be accepted as a reduction.
An evidence write failure stops the campaign explicitly. Consequently the
result is a smaller reproducer, not a claim of
global minimality. Exact coordinate fixtures currently have no specialized
shrinking rules; their original evidence is still preserved.

The routine suite also checks repeatability, strict shrink progress, retention
of the original case, and rejection of reductions that change the failure
class. These are tests of a finite implementation on the kernel version
recorded by the native run. They are neither a proof for all expressions nor
evidence for an untested Wolfram version. The analytic results elsewhere in
this article and numerical or interval certificates have separate evidence
roles.
